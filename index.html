Easy steps to get you started.

We’re excited to have you here. Follow the steps below and you’ll be learning in just a few minutes.

1

Accept your invitation

Check your inbox for the account creation email we already sent you, and click the Accept Invitation button inside it. That takes you straight to the Set Password page.

 
2

Set up your password

Create your account password and confirm it. Choose something secure you’ll remember.

 
3

Log in

With your password set, log in to Chick-fil-A Lean365 using your email and new password.

 
4

Start your Lean journey

After logging in, you’ll land on the LMS homepage where you can browse all available Lean courses.

✓  Click Start on any course to begin.
✓  Select “Ask to join this course” to request access.
 
5

Start learning

Your course teacher will review and approve your request, usually quickly. Once approved, click Start the course and dive right in.

Waiting for approval

Approved, start the course

Begin your first lesson

Have a question or hit a snag?

Don’t sit on it. Our team is ready to help, any time.

1

Raise a ticket

Quickly raise a ticket by clicking the floating chat icon in the bottom-right corner, or by clicking Helpdesk from your left side menu.

 
2

Send it to us

Tell us what’s wrong or ask a question. Attach a screenshot or recording if you can, and send it over. Our team will respond as quickly as possible.

We can’t wait to see you thrive in your Lean365 journey!

Need anything else? Email us at support@cfalean365.com. We’re happy to help.
